## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are expected to demonstrate sustained, constructive contributions;
sound technical judgment; respectful review; and familiarity with the project's
security and compatibility promises. Existing maintainers appoint new
maintainers in a public governance issue. The project will move to a voting
model once it has at least three active maintainers.

## Inactivity and removal

A maintainer may step down at any time. Maintainers who are inactive for six
months may be moved to emeritus status after a public check-in. Removal for a
Code of Conduct or security violation follows the private process described in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

Do not use public issues for vulnerability details or private conduct reports.
Use the channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), respectively.
