# OC4001 — overlapping-exception

**Default severity:** warning

**Category:** exceptions

Reports structural intersections or redundancy among modeled exception scopes.
An omitted `-from` or `-to` is conservatively treated as an unrestricted side.

Emitted severity depends on the combination:

- error: false-path with multicycle, or clock-group cut with multicycle;
- note: false-path redundant with a clock-group cut;
- warning: two exceptions of the same modeled kind overlap.

Narrow the collections or keep one constraint that clearly states the timing
policy. Evidence includes both source locations and bounded from/to
intersections.

The beta models `-from`, `-to`, and coarse `-through` intersections for false,
multicycle, min/max-delay, and clock-group exceptions. It does not reproduce
full STA precedence, edge qualifiers, through ordering, path enumeration, or
formal feasibility. A non-finding does not prove exceptions are compatible.
