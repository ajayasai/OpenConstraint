# OC4001 — overlapping-exception

**Default severity:** warning

**Category:** exceptions

Reports structural intersections or redundancy among modeled exception scopes.
An omitted `-from` or `-to` is conservatively treated as an unrestricted side.
A specified side or through collection that resolves empty makes the scope
unresolvable and is excluded from overlap comparison; its query still produces
the applicable query diagnostic.

Emitted severity depends on the combination:

- error: false-path with multicycle, or clock-group cut with multicycle;
- note: false-path redundant with a clock-group cut;
- warning: two exceptions of the same modeled kind overlap.

Narrow the collections or keep one constraint that clearly states the timing
policy. Evidence includes both source locations and bounded from/to
intersections.

Exception records retain edge-qualified from/to selectors and ordered
edge-qualified through collections for false, multicycle, and min/max-delay
constraints. The overlap decision requires compatible setup/hold analysis
senses, from/to/end transitions, and ordered through sequences. Two non-empty
through sequences are compatible only when the shorter can be embedded in the
longer in order with intersecting objects and edge qualifiers at each matched
step. A clock-group entry participates as a cut only when it has a modeled
relation and does not use `-allow_paths`.

The rule remains a structural approximation: it does not reproduce full STA
precedence, enumerate paths, or prove formal feasibility. A multicycle
`-reset_path` removes earlier exact-scope multicycle candidates before overlap
analysis, matching the modeled command-history semantics. A non-finding does
not prove exceptions are compatible.
