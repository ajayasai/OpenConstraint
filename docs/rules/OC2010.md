# OC2010 — generated-clock-source-missing

**Default severity:** error

**Category:** generated clocks

Reports a generated clock whose `-source` is absent or resolves to no modeled
pin or port.

Point `-source` at the real master-clock path, correct hierarchy/query spelling,
and confirm the source exists in the structural netlist. A zero-object query may
also produce OC1001 at the same location.

The beta requires a statically resolvable source. Tcl-computed source
collections are not executed and will produce query diagnostics.
