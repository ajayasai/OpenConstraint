# OC4010 — invalid-multicycle

**Default severity:** error

**Category:** exceptions

Reports a `set_multicycle_path` with a missing or non-integer multiplier, a
setup multiplier below 1, a hold multiplier below 0, or simultaneous `-start`
and `-end`. With neither `-setup` nor `-hold`, or with both flags, the normalized
constraint applies to both checks.

Use an integer multiplier and at most one start/end edge-reference option, then
state setup and hold intent explicitly. Other rules may still report pairing or
conflict problems after the command shape is valid.

This check does not derive launch/capture edge relationships from clock
waveforms or prove that the multicycle exception is functionally justified.
