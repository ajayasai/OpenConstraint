# OC4002 — invalid-exception-definition

**Default severity:** error

**Category:** exceptions

Reports a modeled timing exception or clock-group command whose static
definition is structurally invalid. For `set_false_path`,
`set_multicycle_path`, `set_max_delay`, and `set_min_delay`, the checks include:

- at least one explicit `-from`, `-through`, or `-to` scope, unless a modeled
  end-transition selector supplies the scope;
- at most one of `-from`, `-rise_from`, and `-fall_from`;
- at most one of `-to`, `-rise_to`, and `-fall_to`;
- every explicitly supplied selector must resolve to a nonempty modeled
  collection, and every leaf of a literal object scope must resolve; an empty,
  malformed, or partly unresolved scope is never narrowed or treated as
  omitted; and
- exactly one finite numeric value for a min/max-delay command.

For `set_clock_groups`, the command must select exactly one modeled
relationship, contain at least two nonempty groups, and keep every clock in at
most one group. Invalid definitions are retained as diagnostics but excluded
from structural overlap analysis, so a malformed exception cannot masquerade
as an unrestricted valid cut.

Correct the command shape and confirm every selector or exact literal scope
resolves to its intended objects. Separate query diagnostics identify empty or
unsupported selectors; `OC4010` covers multiplier and start/end errors specific
to multicycle paths.
OC4002 does not model complete vendor precedence, enumerate timing paths, or
prove that an otherwise well-formed exception is functionally valid.
