# OC1002 — dangerously-broad-query

**Default severity:** warning

**Category:** queries

Reports a glob or regular-expression query whose collection is large enough to
change silently as synthesis or hierarchy evolves.

By default, the universe must contain at least five objects and the query must
match either at least 50 objects or at least 80% of the universe. Configure the
two exposed thresholds with `--broad-match-count` and `--broad-match-ratio`.
`all_inputs`, `all_outputs`, and `all_clocks` are intentionally exempt.

Review the evidence sample, narrow the pattern, or document and regression-test
the exact intended collection. A broad query is not automatically wrong; this
rule identifies change risk, especially for exceptions.

The beta evaluates its supported Python glob/regular-expression semantics, not
every vendor Tcl collection nuance.
