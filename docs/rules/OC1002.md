# OC1002 — dangerously-broad-query

**Default severity:** warning

**Category:** queries

Reports a glob or regular-expression query whose collection is large enough to
change silently as synthesis or hierarchy evolves. The non-regexp grammar uses
only `*` and `?` as wildcards; bracket text is literal. `-regexp` is anchored to
the full comparison name and limited to the documented Tcl-ARE/Python common
subset.

By default, the universe must contain at least five objects and the query must
match either at least 50 objects or at least 80% of the universe. Configure the
two exposed thresholds with `--broad-match-count` and `--broad-match-ratio`.
Intentional `all_*` collection commands, including `all_registers`, are
exempt. A bare supported `get_*` command is not exempt: pinned OpenSTA defines
its omitted pattern as `*`, so OpenConstraint preserves that behavior and
audits the resulting collection for broad-match risk.

Review the evidence sample, narrow the pattern, or document and regression-test
the exact intended collection. A broad query is not automatically wrong; this
rule identifies change risk, especially for exceptions.

Python/Tcl-specific regular-expression extensions, alphanumeric escapes,
backreferences, counted/modified quantifiers, advanced bracket classes, and
non-ASCII case folding are not guessed; they produce `OC1004`. See the
compatibility document for exact regexp routing, current-scope hierarchy, and
`-nocase` behavior.
